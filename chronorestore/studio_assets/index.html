How we measured it

Each image was center cropped to 256 × 256 and corrupted with a fixed seed. The Detail checkpoint adds fine-tuning on the BSDS500 training split using pixel and edge losses. Refine averages four predictions in flipped orientations. Results apply to simulated damage.

What published work adds

Wan et al. · CVPR 2020

Microsoft Research Asia and collaborators used separate paths for structured defects and noise, plus real-photo domain translation. Their DIV2K result was 23.33 dB PSNR and 0.69 SSIM.

Read the paper ↗

Pik-Fix · WACV 2023

Xu et al. combined repair with reference-guided colorization. They reported 17.20 dB and 0.758 SSIM on RealOld, using expert restorations as the targets.

Read the paper ↗

The paper scores use different tests. ChronoRestore’s strongest controlled result is its improvement over the original checkpoint. Fine textures still look softer than the reference, and performance on historical scans remains unmeasured.
